## 2A) High-level structure
Estimated structure: 15 narrative chapters, 3 appendix chapters, 6 appendix sections.

### Chapter loglines
1. **The Return**: Cassian wakes in Hive Spindle Nine, attacks on instinct, then collapses as staff stabilize him.
2. **Before the Jump**: Launch-day flashback sets mission context and introduces the anomaly warning.
3. **A Flaw in the Pattern**: Korin identifies Cassian as an impossible temporal outlier and begins investigation.
4. **The Walk**: Cassian regains movement and has a guarded first read of Korin.
5. **The Opening Move**: Korin briefs 2416-era Hive reality and probes Cassian's memory.
6. **Controlled Variables**: Rehabilitation becomes coercive extraction under Alyen.
7. **The Seam**: A Reversionist-assisted escape breaks Cassian out via maintenance routes.
8. **The Drop**: Cassian survives the shaft, relives alien contact, and hears "Stop the expansion."
9. **---**: Cassian reaches neglected infrastructure and gains temporary separation from patrols.
10. **Signal to Noise**: Korin and Alyen clash on method; Cassian is recast as strategic anomaly.
11. **Contingencies**: Cassian contacts a damaged remnant of his ship AI.
12. **The Lanes Between Signals**: Cassian follows non-Hive guidance through undergrid comm routes.
13. **The Soft Edge**: The AI shard confirms it survived with Cassian and warns of larger risk.
14. **The Broken Clinic**: Alyen reviews footage and reassesses Cassian as highly structured threat.
15. **The Night the Oath Broke**: Korin pivots to indirect pursuit; Alyen's certainty cracks.
16. **Character Dossiers**: Meta profiles define Cassian, Alyen, and Gor.
17. **Timeline and Structural Summary**: Meta recap of Chapters 1-15.
18. **World and Lore Notes**: Canon notes on Hive structure, Reversionists, and anomaly facts.

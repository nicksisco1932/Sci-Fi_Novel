\chapter{A Flaw in the Pattern}

Korin Tal did not like anomalies.

Not because they were dangerous, but because they meant something had slipped—an unaccounted variable.

Now one stood recorded before him: a human who should have been dust.

“Unscheduled biological signature. Temporal mismatch,” the pillar reported.

Korin contacted Spindle Nine.

“Subject sedated. No casualties,” came the response.

“No casualties,” he repeated. “Comforting.”

He requested telemetry. Neural scans. Overlays.

Sixteen seconds of delay. Unacceptable.

In his chamber, fractal reinforcement patterns steadied his thoughts.

An outline flickered at the corner of the interface.

Unclassified.

Outdated.

Impossible.

The system identified it with a name unused for four centuries:

Cassian Rho.

\chapter{Before the Jump}

The sky above Earth was still blue back then.

Cassian sat at the edge of the launch pad’s pre-departure station, watching clouds shift over the Pacific Array. Launch towers bristled like metallic needles against the curvature of the planet.

A technician approached. “You’re cleared for Phase Three. Vitals optimal. Neural mesh stable.”

He smirked. “Only matters if I come back.”

“You will,” she said. “You’re too stubborn not to.”

He walked toward the Helion transfer pod. Engineers moved with quiet precision, each acknowledging Cassian as the point of humanity’s reach.

The AI activated as he entered the cradle.

“Cassian Rho. Helion-class vessel online. Begin interface check.”

“Confirmed.”

“Overlay locked. Quantum relay ready. Hyperspace gate stable.”

“All green.”

Lights pulsed. Systems synchronized with his breathing.

“Cassian,” the AI said softly.

He paused.

“There is an anomaly in the destination field.”

“Show me.”

Darkness swallowed the response.

Then—

The hiss of a translation device in Hive Spindle Nine.

Cassian jerked awake on the cot, memory dragging him forward.

# Volume One Style Review

Status: art sequence locked for proof assembly; lettering remains a separate production gate.

## Review Basis

- Forty-two story pages viewed together in the 48-page contact sheet.
- Chapter 1 continuity sheet used as foundational craft authority.
- Accepted location and scene references used as contextual authorities for lighting, weather, palette, and camera.

## Findings

- **The Return:** clinical pages establish the clean line, anatomical, and material baseline. The new Korin office page and selected rain meditation coda extend that grammar into executive darkness without changing his identity.
- **Controlled Variables:** the bright rehabilitation sequence remains intentionally constrained and provides a stable visual control before the escape.
- **Lanes Between Signals:** the shaft, alien memory, Lower Grid, Central Cognition, planetary map, Spindle 31, and market pages broaden scale and palette while retaining readable figures and physical environments.
- **The Night the Oath Broke:** rain, clinic, safehouse, dojo, processing chamber, and burning Almelah form the broadest contextual range. Character identity and panel clarity remain sufficient for the proof.

## Locked Decisions

- Use `variant-03-the-rain.png` as the Korin meditation coda.
- End Volume One on Gor's rain epilogue after Chapter 15.
- Use continuous section titles rather than manuscript chapter numbers.
- Preserve contextual differences instead of forcing every page into Chapter 1's pale clinical palette.
- Treat existing accepted pages as immutable sources; proof sequence files are derived copies.

## Non-Blocking Risks

- The Earth integration page is more environmental and photoreal in scale than character-led pages, but the shift is motivated and readable.
- Lower Grid pages are materially dense; lettering must remain sparse and avoid covering hands, terminals, Walker's gait, or the medical-drone decision.
- Early pages contain generated lettering and remain subject to the existing one-page dialogue correction queue.

No visual rerender is required before the first complete proof PDF. Publication approval remains blocked on the lettering audit.

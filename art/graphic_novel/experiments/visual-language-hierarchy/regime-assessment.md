# Accepted Visual-Regime Assessment

Baseline commit: `95876ac`

This assessment separates stable craft requirements from context-dependent visual language. It does not reclassify, replace, or modify accepted assets.

## Chapter 1 Wake-Up - Foundational Control

Exact reference:

- `art/graphic_novel/chapter_01_wakeup/continuity-sheet.png`

Stable authority:

- Restrained, deliberate linework.
- Credible anatomy and hand geometry.
- Specific medical materials and controlled surface rendering.
- Coherent perspective and readable blocking.
- Finished graphic-novel illustration rather than loose concept art.

Contextual qualities:

- Pale clinical palette, diffuse sterile light, low environmental contamination, and measured panel rhythm belong to the recovery room.
- These qualities provide the experiment's control condition but should not become mandatory for every environment.

## Chapter 14 Broken Clinic - Dark Interior Authority

Exact references:

- `art/graphic_novel/scenes/chapter_14_broken_clinic/page-01.png`
- `art/graphic_novel/locations/broken-clinic/landscape-reference.png`
- `art/graphic_novel/characters/broken-clinic-leader-and-guard/continuity-sheet.png`
- `art/graphic_novel/characters/cassian-rho/continuity-sheet.png`
- `art/graphic_novel/characters/thena/continuity-sheet.png`

Stable authority:

- Cassian, Thena, and clinic personnel retain approved identity and proportions.
- Damp metal, patched barriers, med-kit surfaces, clothing, and skin remain materially legible.
- The corridor and clinic retain physical depth and practical construction.

Contextual qualities:

- Warm, impure practical lamps against brown-black shadow.
- Dense patched texture, coolant damp, compressed framing, and constricted sightlines.
- Low-key exposure with enough separation to read faces, hands, wounds, and equipment.

Hypothesis:

Treating Chapter 1 as craft authority while allowing the clinic to govern local contrast and texture should preserve finish without bleaching the scene or reducing its pressure.

## Chapter 13 Rain Ambush - Harsh-Weather Authority

Exact references:

- `art/graphic_novel/scenes/chapter_13_soft_edge/page-01.png`
- `art/graphic_novel/locations/rain-sector-ambush/landscape-reference.png`
- `art/graphic_novel/characters/gor-of-almelah/continuity-sheet.png`
- `art/graphic_novel/characters/cassian-rho/continuity-sheet.png`
- `art/graphic_novel/characters/thena/continuity-sheet.png`

Stable authority:

- Character identities, augmentation geometry, anatomy, impact physics, and material specificity.
- Spatially coherent wall breach, overhang, wet deck, and combat blocking.

Contextual qualities:

- Cold blue-gray weather, black wet metal, sharp lightning, directional rain, and hard specular separation.
- More dynamic camera placement and panel rhythm than the wake-up control.
- High contrast remains bounded by readable action and non-superhero physical behavior.

Hypothesis:

The weather reference should be allowed to intensify palette, contrast, and rhythm while Chapter 1 continues to govern drawing discipline and physical credibility.

## Hive City Exterior - Large-Scale Environmental Authority

Exact references:

- `art/graphic_novel/locations/hive-spindle-city/landscape-reference.png`
- `art/graphic_novel/locations/hive-earth/visual-brief.md`

Stable authority:

- Materially convincing architecture, coherent perspective, controlled detail, and finished illustration.
- Canon scale, civilizational function, and physical inhabitation remain primary.

Contextual qualities:

- High-altitude camera, atmospheric perspective, pale natural daylight, enormous depth, and environmental rather than character-led composition.
- Scale requires broad value grouping and distant detail behavior that would be inappropriate in the medical control sheet.

Hypothesis:

An immersive location plate cannot be judged by the wake-up scene's palette or panel rhythm. It can still share its precision, restraint, and finish.

## Pre-Render Finding

The accepted production set already contains multiple visual regimes. The current experiment tests whether explicitly naming the hierarchy improves prompt control: Chapter 1 remains the foundational craft authority, while accepted scene and location references govern atmosphere, local cinematography, and panel behavior.

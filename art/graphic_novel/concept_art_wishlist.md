# Black-and-White Concept Art Wishlist

Purpose: turn the canonical entity inventory into an efficient inspiration queue without treating exploratory art as canon.

Coverage source: `docs/obsidian_wikilink_report.md`. That report supplies the subject list, not visual instructions. Before rendering, use the source order in `art/graphic_novel/SOP.md`: canonical manuscript, lore registry, timeline, dossiers, style guide, then existing art.

## Production Contract

- Produce exactly **two sketches per subject**: Concept A and Concept B.
- Both images are monochrome: black ink, graphite, charcoal gray, and white paper only. No color accents.
- Treat these as loose inspiration sketches, not final continuity locks. Creative variation is welcome inside established canon.
- Make A and B genuinely different design ideas, not the same composition from another camera angle.
- Use clean silhouettes, readable materials, controlled hatching, and deliberate negative space. Avoid muddy concept-art density.
- No invented dialogue, labels, slogans, insignia, faction uniforms, or explanatory text inside the image.
- Preserve ambiguity around unrevealed people, ancient factions, and disputed history. When visual permission is weak, sketch material culture, spaces, records, or aftermath rather than fixing a face or symbol.
- Existing art is an anchor, not a reason to skip the item. New sketches should explore a useful alternate staging or design emphasis without contradicting the approved reference.
- Default canvas: character `4:5` portrait; location/institution/event `3:2` landscape; technology `3:2` reference board.
- Default output: `art/graphic_novel/concept_sketches/<category>/<slug>/concept-a.png` and `concept-b.png`.
- After both sketches exist, review them as `A`, `B`, `combine`, or `defer`. Only a later continuity pass can promote an idea into the asset registry.

## Efficient Batch Order

1. **Batch 1 — Production gaps:** locations and technology already marked as needed in the asset registry.
2. **Batch 2 — Core visual language:** characters and places with approved references, using the sketches to widen inspiration without redesigning continuity.
3. **Batch 3 — Institutions and deep history:** atmospheric, symbol-free concepts that preserve uncertainty.
4. **Batch 4 — Selection pass:** compare every A/B pair, record promising elements, and promote only approved choices to formal reference work.

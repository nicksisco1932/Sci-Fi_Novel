# Graphic Novel Asset Registry

Purpose: track available graphic-novel assets and decide what needs rendering next without rereading the whole manuscript every time.

Use `art/graphic_novel/SOP.md` for status meanings, directory layout, and generation workflow. This registry is a production tracker, not canon.

Dialogue approval for the initial pages is tracked separately in `art/graphic_novel/dialogue_correction_todo.md`. An image marked `Available` here is not publication-ready until its dialogue row is `Approved` there.

Scene production for Chapters 11-22 is catalogued in `art/graphic_novel/remaining_scene_catalogue.md`, including completed sequences, priorities, required continuity locks, and recommended render batches.

Exploratory two-sketch-per-subject work is tracked separately in `art/graphic_novel/concept_art_wishlist.md`. Those black-and-white sketches are inspiration material and do not become continuity assets until reviewed and promoted here.

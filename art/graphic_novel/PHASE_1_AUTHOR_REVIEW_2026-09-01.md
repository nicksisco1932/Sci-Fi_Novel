Purpose: provide one fast, stable entry point for the Phase 1 candidates that still require an author decision. The images remain in their production directories; this page links and previews them without moving or duplicating any asset.

## How to Review Quickly

1. View each image at normal page scale.
2. Enter one number in the `Answer` cell for question `D` (Disposition).
3. If `D` is `1` or `2`, move to the next image. The remaining questions are optional.
4. If `D` is `3`, `4`, or `5`, answer only the specific rows that explain the desired change.
5. Add prose under `Optional note` only when the numbered choices cannot express the direction.

### Universal Disposition Scale

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| `1` | Accept and use as-is. |
| `2` | Accept now; keep a minor issue on the later-polish watchlist. |
| `3` | Make one focused correction, then use. |
| `4` | Make several focused corrections before use. |
| `5` | Defer, rebuild, or replace. |

The other scales are deliberately tailored to the question. A higher number does not always mean “better”; it may mean darker, curlier, brighter, more emotional, or more extensive intervention.

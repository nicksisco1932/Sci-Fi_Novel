# Surface Age Silo Stack Visual Brief

Status: adaptation reference only; `Surface Age stack field` is an art-production label, not a canon location name.

## Source Boundaries

- `docs/lore_registry.md` establishes the Surface Age and the Great Sorrow as deep-history context involving heat, infrastructure failure, disaster pressure, and institutional breakdown.
- `docs/appendices/Appendix – Figures of the Turning Time.md` establishes extraction, offshore drilling, disaster reconstruction, environmental relief, and acid rain during the Voss-era crisis.
- The approved stack style reference supplies the storm shelf, flooded horizon, monumental vertical rhythm, and muted yellow-gray light.
- The approved Hive references supply geographic scale and high-altitude perspective only. They do not make the Surface Age stacks part of the later Hive system.

## Adaptation Design

- The definitive stack-field image contains exactly one Surface Age industrial megasilo, not a chimney bundle, tower cluster, city, or repeated field of towers.
- The silo is a broad cylindrical shaft approximately four times the diameter of the earlier needle design. It rises from a restrained low footing and extends into the storm cloud deck.
- The remainder of the flooded plain is empty water, mud flat, salt crust, drowned terrain, and storm shadow without intermediate development or distant tower silhouettes.
- The stacks are older, darker, more exposed, and more materially exhausted than the pale sealed Hive. Their relationship to the Hive is one of construction scale, not shared architecture or confirmed historical descent.

## Visual Guardrails

- Preserve broad water geography, storm systems, rain curtains, pale vapor, and restrained yellow-gray light.
- Use cloud penetration, rain walls, the restrained footing, and the uninterrupted wasteland around the single silo as scale indicators.
- Keep the right side dark and atmospheric. Any break in the left cloud shelf should remain subdued and approximately twenty percent dimmer than the original stack reference.
- Avoid additional or distant silos, ordinary power plants, grouped towers, chimney bundles, dense industrial carpets, city-like bases, modern skylines, residential windows, gothic or religious architecture, bright data grids, cyberpunk neon, war damage, and apocalypse spectacle.
- Do not add labels, corporate marks, faction insignia, or readable text.

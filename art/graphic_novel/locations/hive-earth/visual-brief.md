# Hive Earth Visual Brief

Status: adaptation reference only; not manuscript canon.

## Canon Sources

- `docs/canonical_md/Chapter 1 – The Return.md`, lines 155-175: the Hive city beyond the glass wall, including stacked districts, elevation rails, disciplined white towers, functional light bands, spindle-crown vapor, pressure-sealed avenues, and treated rain.
- `docs/canonical_md/Chapter 11 – Contingencies.md`, lines 49-85: Earth redrawn by signal strength and system integration, with seamless core regions and increasingly broken services around Spindles 22 and 31 and the Polar Grids.

## Canon Requirements

- **Hive Earth** is a planet-spanning civilizational system. A **Spindle** is one physical expression of that system, not the whole Hive.
- Core Hive architecture is pale, aligned, highly integrated, and friction-managed rather than decorative.
- A major Hive structure must read as a deep, layered urban volume capable of housing approximately 25 million people. Its physical mass continues far beyond the brightly integrated center.
- Residents follow calculated prompts and assigned routines. Their movement should feel subdued and anticipatory, not like shopping, leisure, military marching, or mindless horror.
- The planetary system covers Earth unevenly through distinct Hive urban bodies, transport corridors, relays, and service reach. Oceans, continents, atmosphere, weather, and polar geography remain visible between them.
- Network inequality is shown through continuity, density, service response, dead nodes, and gaps rather than national borders or moral color coding.

## Adaptation Decisions

- Each major Hive is represented as a broad engineered urban plateau supporting several pale spindle crowns, with a buried inhabited body deeper than its visible skyline.
- The exterior uses white and silver composite shells, exposed infrastructure bands, enclosed rail corridors, vapor vents, and restrained amber lower-level lighting to extend the approved Chapter 1 interior language.
- Planetary views show multiple discrete Hives visible through atmosphere. Integrated core regions use continuous physical transport corridors; peripheral and polar regions use sparse, interrupted routes and isolated service nodes.
- Visible service lighting is densest near integrated centers and progressively thins outward and downward while the inhabited structure continues into haze and shadow.
- The supplied external megacity references informed only planetary footprint, vertical depth, and population scale. Their proprietary architecture, gothic ornament, militarism, text, logos, and imagery are excluded.
- Exact Hive footprints, tower silhouettes, rail geometry, underground layering, network placement, and orbital viewpoint remain adaptation designs until separately approved as canon.

## Prohibited Drift

- No literal hive, honeycomb, hexagon, insect, or swarm imagery.
- No metal-encased Earth, planetary machine shell, national borders, flags, faction insignia, or readable interface labels.
- No gothic, medieval, religious, imperial, or franchise-derived architecture.
- No cyberpunk neon, generic dystopian grime, pristine utopian advertising, empty ruins, zombie-like crowds, shopping behavior, or military formations.
- Do not treat all Hive regions as equally functional or equally degraded.

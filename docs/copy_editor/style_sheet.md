## Establish from the selected text

Record actual examples for spelling variants, capitalization of institutional terms, compounds, hyphenation, numerals, dialogue interruption marks, and interface formatting. Do not assume a national spelling convention or external style manual was approved. Treat narration, dialogue, and interface wording separately. A repeated form supports a consistency query; repetition alone does not prove correctness.

Distinguish deliberate rhetorical repetition from duplicated-word typos, purposeful fragments from missing syntax, dialect from agreement errors, and viewpoint ambiguity from an accidental unclear antecedent. Record uncertainty instead of manufacturing a correction.

# Korin Tal

## Canonical Role
Korin Tal is a loyalist figure inside Hive Earth and an architect-level analyst of Hive cognition. He operates through the Central Cognition Sector and Spindle 9's cognitive-control infrastructure.

He is not Alyen's blunt instrument. He is the colder strategic mind above her: controlled, patient, and more dangerous when he appears reasonable.

## Core Drive
Korin wants control, system preservation, and restoration of predictive order.

Cassian Rho matters to him because Cassian is not only a fugitive. He is proof that something can enter Hive Earth unindexed, survive outside its models, and expose the system's dependency loops by existing beyond them.

## Behavioral Profile
- Controlled charisma; warmth used as pressure rather than kindness.
- Austere authority; rarely raises his voice.
- Lets silence work before speaking.
- Frames interrogation as conversation, recovery, rehabilitation, or assimilation.
- Treats anomalies as structural problems, not emotional provocations.
- Watches for what people hide by studying what they do not ask.

## Relationship Vectors
- Cassian Rho: Strategic anomaly and psychological opponent. Korin probes rather than assaults, because Cassian's value lies in what he survived and what he may remember.
- Alyen Ithra: Loyal enforcer and escalation tool. Alyen believes in Korin's vision, but her need for certainty makes her less precise than he is.
- Hive Earth: Korin serves the system and its continuity, but he is perceptive enough to see its cracks before others admit they exist.
- Reversionist: An unseen rival pattern. Korin recognizes that Cassian's escape implies an intelligence or actor moving through infrastructure gaps the Hive failed to close.

## Story Function
Korin makes the Hive feel intelligent rather than merely oppressive. He embodies the system's ability to observe, classify, absorb, and wait.

His pressure should stay procedural and elegant. He is most threatening when he names the correct problem softly.

## Continuity Anchors
- Full name: Korin Tal.
- Short form after identification: Korin.
- Canon role: loyalist figure; control and system preservation.
- Operational domain: Central Cognition Sector, Spindle 9, Hive cognitive infrastructure.
- Early arc: identifies Cassian as a temporal and structural anomaly, probes his memories indirectly, then escalates through Alyen when controlled methods are insufficient.

## Do Not Change
- Do not turn Korin into an overt sadist.
- Do not make him careless, loud, or impulsive.
- Do not make him omniscient; he infers patterns from evidence and system gaps.
- Do not collapse him into Alyen's role. Alyen enforces; Korin interprets and directs.

# Todo Idea Log

This file is not canon by itself. Treat entries as prompts until they are worked into `docs/canonical_md/`, the lore registry, or another authoritative document.

## Status Key
- Open: available for future use.
- In progress: actively being drafted or placed.
- Integrated: worked into a source file; include the file/chapter pointer.
- Closed: rejected, superseded, or no longer useful.

## Use Rules
1. Check this file at the start of each repo work session.
2. Do not force an idea into an unrelated edit.
3. Before integrating an idea into manuscript prose, read `docs/lore_registry.md`, `docs/canonical_md/Current Timeline Summary.md`, and the relevant chapter or character dossier.
4. If an idea creates or changes a canon term, update `docs/lore_registry.md` first.
5. Keep implementation surgical and consistent with the house style in `AGENTS.md`.

### Universe Technology: Living substrate
- Status: Dreaming / non-canon. "Living substrate" is working shorthand, not a canon name.
- Characters: Civilization-scale human populations; Cassian as witness to a possible parallel, not a chosen key or necessary bridge.
- Place / time: An emergent layer inside Hive-era Harmony and Cognitive Architecture after human connection passes from social media and behavioral prediction through neural implants into planetary-scale integration.
- Core possibility: A Type I civilization has a real coordination problem. Planetary energy, climate, logistics, defense, and transit cannot be managed quickly enough through conventional institutions. Loss of individual freedom is not inherently required, but the Hive solution progressively converts connection into control.
- Human infrastructure: At sufficient implant density, small fragments of neural processing are recruited from millions or billions of people. No participant receives enough of a problem to interpret it. The network recombines unconscious pattern recognition into civilization-scale calculations, making human minds the physical processing substrate of a planetary machine.
- Awareness: Ordinary people know they are connected but generally do not know when or how their cognition is being used. Possible effects include synchronized dreams, regional exhaustion, missing attention, mass distraction, or millions of people reaching the same conclusion without remembering where it began.
- Surface peace / hidden war: Prediction, coordination, and behavioral regulation reduce open warfare on the civilized surface. Conflict persists inside the substrate as factions compete for routing priority, cognitive bandwidth, human nodes, and access to planetary calculations. A victory might decide whose climate model is trusted, which fleet moves first, which crisis receives attention, or which future the system begins preparing to make inevitable.
- Scope guardrail: This processing conflict is one form of war in the universe, not the explanation for every political, military, or cultural struggle.
- Quantum connection: The beings Cassian encountered may use the same underlying principle in a mature, organic, or naturally evolved form. Thousands of distinct entities delivered one cognition whole without behaving like a conventional broadcast network. Humanity may be converging on the same phenomenon, crudely imitating it, or touching something both species discovered independently.
- Cassian connection: Cassian's encounter gives him evidence through which he may later recognize the pattern. It does not grant him unique control. Preserve the ambiguity of "Stop the expansion": territorial expansion, network density, cognitive assimilation, or something larger may be meant.
- Materiality: Operators understand observable inputs and effects better than the emergent whole, allowing the technology to feel arcane without becoming magic. Physical implants, energy, signal infrastructure, density, and routing remain necessary.
- Firm provisional limits: No unrestricted mind reading. No omnipotent behavioral control. No consequence-free instantaneous communication. The mechanism used by the quantum beings remains unknown.
- Canon questions to check:
  - What physical threshold causes an implant network to become an emergent living substrate?
  - Is human neural participation merely efficient, or does consciousness provide something nonliving processors cannot reproduce?
  - Who first notices that unexplained fatigue and synchronized behavior correlate with major computation?
  - Can people refuse processing, and what happens when enough nodes disconnect?
  - Do blind zones fall outside the substrate, or does human proximity allow weaker connection without intact infrastructure?
  - Can factions commandeer processing without also changing the people performing it?
  - Does coercion introduce noise, fragmentation, or hostile emergent behavior?
  - Is the substrate becoming conscious, or are humans assigning identity to distributed computation?
- Existing anchors to preserve: Cassian's neural mesh, the quantum entanglement relay, Harmony, Cognitive Architecture, blind zones, the beings' collective contact, and the warning "Stop the expansion." Do not change their established meanings while this idea remains in Dreaming.
- Development pointer: The communication trajectory, component stack, and provisional maturity model are explored in `docs/developmental/Hive Communication – Convergence Model.md` and `reports/notebooks/hive_earth_systems_stress_test.ipynb`. This pointer does not change the entry's Dreaming / non-canon status.

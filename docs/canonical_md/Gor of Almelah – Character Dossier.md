 Gor of Almelah – Character Dossier
Name: Gor of Almelah
Role: Hive-aligned time-displaced assassin / hunter
Origins: Warrior tradition of Almelah, trained in the same dojo as Cassian Rho
Cultural Background – Almelah
Frontier warrior culture with a strict code.
Core tenet:
No augmentation
No lifespan extension
No cryo-sleep
No “unnatural” interference with aging
Oath: Live your span. Die your span. Do not prolong your shadow.
Relationship to Cassian
Trained in the same dojo under the same master.
Sparred dozens of times through their youth.
Cassian consistently won, but with respect, precision, and control.
Gor carried a private vow: one day, he would surpass Cassian.
Oath-Breaking Arc
After Cassian left for the Helion mission, the Hive came for Almelah.
Offered Gor augmentation and cryo capability in exchange for service.
Initially refused as an oath-bound traditionalist.
Under Hive pressure and orchestrated crisis, chose enhancement to “save” what he could.
Became a long-span operative, able to cycle in and out of cryo as a reusable weapon.
First major mission: lead a purge of Almelah’s remaining resistance.
Personally killed members of his own order, including their master.
This act, not the surgery, is the true breaking of his oath.
Present-Day Psychology
Sees himself as a purified version of the old warrior ideal: stripped of illusion.
Believes softness, hesitation, and sentiment are moral failures.
Sees Cassian as:
The rival who always beat him.
The man who left, then somehow returned centuries later.
Living proof of a path Gor abandoned.
He doesn’t just want Cassian dead; he wants Cassian measured and judged.
